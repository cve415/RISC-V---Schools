## Overview

This document tracks Philippine colleges and universities by their level of RISC-V integration across all regions. The Philippine engineering and CS ecosystem is early-stage relative to RISC-V adoption — Tier 1 entries are sparse, and most programs represent strong Tier 3 and Tier 4 opportunities for curriculum development and partnership outreach.

Coverage spans all regions: NCR, CAR, Regions I–XIII, and BARMM.

## Notes and Verification Guidance

- **UP System priority:** UP Diliman, UP Los Baños, UPM, and UPV should be tracked as a cluster — curriculum decisions at Diliman tend to propagate across campuses. Confirm via the EEEI Department and DOST-funded lab projects.
- **MSU-IIT is the key Mindanao anchor:** If one Mindanao school achieves Tier 1 status, it is most likely MSU-IIT. Prioritize outreach here.
- **CHED alignment:** The Commission on Higher Education's CMO No. 25 (ECE curriculum) and CMO No. 17 (Computer Engineering) define required architecture content. A RISC-V curriculum pitch should map explicitly to these CMOs.
- **DOST–ERDT network:** The Engineering Research and Development for Technology (ERDT) consortium (UP Diliman, DLSU, AdDU, MSU-IIT, and others) is the fastest path to Tier 1 adoption — faculty in this network are most likely to introduce open-ISA tools into graduate and advanced undergraduate courses.
