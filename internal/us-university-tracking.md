## Notes on Seminole State College of Florida

- Seminole State’s **CDA3100 – Introduction to Computer Architecture** lists *“Computer Organization + Design RISC-V Ed.”* as the required textbook in the official catalog, indicating that RISC-V is central to the course content [web:73].  
- Course materials and exercises for CDA3100 include explicit **RISC-V assembly code analysis** and written exercises, showing active, hands-on RISC-V usage in the curriculum [web:74][web:77].  
- A recent paper and supporting materials from Seminole State also discuss RISC-V instruction handling and assembly, reinforcing that RISC-V is embedded in their teaching and not just theoretical [web:78].
