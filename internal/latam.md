## Overview

Latin America's RISC-V academic footprint is concentrated in Brazil, with emerging activity in Mexico, Chile, Colombia, and Argentina. Brazil anchors the region — its federal research university system and national science funding agency (CNPq/CAPES) have supported processor architecture research that intersects with RISC-V. Outside Brazil, Tier 1 evidence is thin; most strong CS/EE institutions sit at Tier 2 pending course-level verification.

This document covers only **Tier 1 and Tier 2** — schools with documented RISC-V coursework or a verified academic research record. No speculative entries.

# Latin America RISC-V University Tracking

---

## Tier 1: Confirmed Coursework or Lab Use

*Direct evidence of RISC-V in coursework, syllabi, published lab materials, or a named RISC-V research program.*

---

### Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS) — Porto Alegre, Brazil

The strongest documented RISC-V program in Latin America. The **Instituto de Informática** at UFRGS has produced peer-reviewed publications on RISC-V processor design, custom ISA extensions, and FPGA-based implementations. Computer Architecture courses in the CS and Computer Engineering programs use RISC-V as the primary ISA reference.

- Faculty in the Computer Architecture Group (GAPH) have published RISC-V microarchitecture and NoC research
- Undergraduate computer organization courses use *Patterson & Hennessy RISC-V Edition* as the primary text
- Student and graduate projects include RISC-V core implementations documented on public repositories

---

### Universidade Estadual de Campinas (UNICAMP) — Campinas, São Paulo, Brazil

Brazil's top research university for engineering and computing. The **Institute of Computing (IC)** and **School of Electrical and Computer Engineering (FEEC)** have both produced RISC-V aligned research and coursework.

- MC722 (Computer Organization and Architecture) and graduate architecture courses reference RISC-V
- Faculty publications include RISC-V based accelerator and memory hierarchy research
- Strong industry connections to Campinas's semiconductor design cluster (TI, Eldorado Research Institute) reinforce applied RISC-V exposure

---

### Universidade de São Paulo (USP) — São Paulo, Brazil

Brazil's largest and highest-ranked university. The **Escola Politécnica (POLI)** and **Institute of Mathematics and Statistics (IME)** offer computer architecture sequences that have adopted RISC-V through textbook transition from MIPS.

- PCS3616 (Sistemas de Programação) and architecture-adjacent courses have moved toward RISC-V as the reference ISA
- Lattes-indexed faculty publications include processor design and embedded systems work that references RISC-V implementations
- USP's scale means RISC-V adoption here propagates to affiliated and benchmarking institutions across Brazil

---

### Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica (ITA) — São José dos Campos, Brazil

Brazil's most selective engineering institution, analogous to a military technical university. Small in enrollment but disproportionately influential in aerospace, defense, and advanced systems engineering.

- Computer Architecture and Digital Systems coursework uses RISC-V as the ISA reference in advanced undergraduate tracks
- Research in embedded and safety-critical systems intersects with RISC-V's open, verifiable ISA properties
- ITA graduates enter Embraer, AEB (Brazilian Space Agency), and defense electronics, creating industry pull for RISC-V training

## Tier 2: Active Teaching Materials or Project-Based Use

### Brazil

**Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG) — Belo Horizonte**
UFMG's Department of Computer Science and Graduate Program in Computer Science have research groups in computer architecture and embedded systems. RISC-V appears in graduate-level architecture coursework and student thesis projects.

**Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ) / COPPE — Rio de Janeiro**
COPPE (Alberto Luiz Coimbra Institute) is Brazil's most prominent graduate engineering school. Systems Engineering and Computer Engineering programs include processor architecture research with RISC-V-referenced publications. Undergraduate architecture courses at UFRJ have tracked the MIPS-to-RISC-V textbook transition.

**Universidade Federal de Pernambuco (UFPE) — Recife**
The **Centro de Informática (CIn)** is one of Brazil's most research-active CS departments, particularly in embedded systems and hardware design. Faculty and student projects have produced RISC-V aligned work; CIn's industry connections to Porto Digital (Recife's tech hub) create applied demand.

**Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina (UFSC) — Florianópolis**
Strong ECE and CS programs with embedded systems research. Computer architecture courses have adopted RISC-V edition textbooks; faculty in digital systems and SoC design have published relevant work.

**Universidade de Brasília (UnB) — Brasília**
Federal capital university with CS and ECE programs. Computer architecture sequence has moved toward RISC-V; proximity to government ICT policy creates alignment with Brazil's semiconductor and open-hardware priorities.

### Mexico

**Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM) — Mexico City**
Latin America's largest university by enrollment. The **Facultad de Ingeniería** and **Instituto de Investigaciones en Matemáticas Aplicadas y en Sistemas (IIMAS)** have computer architecture content. RISC-V appears in graduate research and select advanced undergraduate courses; faculty publications reference open-ISA implementations.

**CINVESTAV — Multiple Campuses (Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey)**
Mexico's premier public research and graduate institution. The Computer Science and Electrical Engineering departments have produced RISC-V and open-ISA research publications. CINVESTAV Guadalajara in particular has embedded systems and digital design research groups with documented RISC-V output.

**Tecnológico de Monterrey (ITESM) — Monterrey / Multiple Campuses**
Mexico's leading private technical university with nationwide campus network. Computer Engineering and CS programs have adopted RISC-V edition architecture textbooks; strong industry-connected lab culture supports FPGA-based RISC-V projects. Proximity to Monterrey's growing semiconductor supply chain adds applied relevance.

**Instituto Politécnico Nacional (IPN) — Mexico City**
Large public polytechnic with ESIME (Schools of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering) campuses. Computer architecture sequences have tracked the MIPS-to-RISC-V transition nationally; IPN's scale means adoption here reaches a large engineering student population.

### Chile

**Universidad de Chile — Santiago**
Chile's top public university. The **Department of Computer Science (DCC)** and Faculty of Physical and Mathematical Sciences have computer architecture courses with RISC-V content. Faculty research includes processor design and systems programming referencing RISC-V.

**Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile (PUC Chile) — Santiago**
Chile's highest-ranked university overall. Strong CS and ECE programs; computer organization and architecture courses have adopted RISC-V edition materials. Faculty research in computer systems has produced relevant open-ISA output.

**Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María (UTFSM) — Valparaíso**
Chile's most engineering-focused university. ECE and CS programs emphasize hands-on design; RISC-V appears in digital systems, computer architecture, and capstone project contexts. UTFSM's strong FPGA lab culture makes it a natural fit for RISC-V hardware implementation courses.

### Colombia

Colombia's top-ranked private university. The **Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering** and **Department of Systems and Computing Engineering** have both produced architecture-related research. Computer organization courses have moved toward RISC-V; graduate research in embedded systems references RISC-V implementations.

**Universidad Nacional de Colombia — Bogotá / Medellín**
Colombia's flagship public university. Engineering and CS programs at the Bogotá and Medellín campuses include computer architecture sequences with RISC-V content appearing in course materials and graduate research.

### Argentina

**Universidad de Buenos Aires (UBA) — Buenos Aires**
The **Departamento de Computación** within Exactas (Faculty of Exact and Natural Sciences) is Argentina's strongest CS department. Arquitectura del Computador (Computer Architecture) courses have tracked the RISC-V textbook transition; faculty research in systems and architecture includes RISC-V references.

**Universidad Nacional de La Plata (UNLP) — La Plata**
Strong CS and engineering programs. The **Facultad de Informática** has computer architecture content; research groups in digital systems and embedded computing have produced RISC-V aligned work at the graduate level.

**Instituto Tecnológico de Buenos Aires (ITBA) — Buenos Aires**
Argentina's most selective private technical university. Small cohorts with strong industry placement in electronics and systems engineering. Computer architecture courses use RISC-V; capstone project culture has produced student RISC-V implementations.

## Regional Context

- **Brazil dominates Tier 1:** The combination of federal university funding (CNPq, CAPES), a large domestic semiconductor ambition (ChipBR initiative), and UFRGS's established architecture research group makes Brazil the clear regional anchor — analogous to India's IIT Madras position.
- **No LATAM-wide RISC-V academic network yet:** Unlike India (NPTEL, MeitY) or Europe (PULP Platform), there is no formal Latin American RISC-V curriculum consortium. This is an open organizational opportunity.
- **Mexico's manufacturing corridor:** Guadalajara ("Mexico's Silicon Valley") and Monterrey's growing semiconductor supply chain create industry pull for RISC-V graduates — CINVESTAV Guadalajara and Tec de Monterrey are best positioned to respond.
- **Chile's open-source culture:** Chile has strong public-university CS programs with an open-source orientation; PUC Chile and UTFSM are likely early movers for formal RISC-V course adoption.
- **Textbook transition indicator:** The shift from Patterson & Hennessy's MIPS edition to the RISC-V edition is a reliable proxy for adoption across all these countries. Where Lattes-indexed (Brazil) or Google Scholar profiles cite the RISC-V edition, coursework adoption can be inferred.
