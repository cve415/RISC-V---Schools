## Overview

This document maps the leading U.S. academic institutions integrating RISC-V into core engineering curricula and advanced research. These institutions are the primary feeders for the next generation of silicon architects and RTL engineers.

# U.S. University RISC-V Integration Tracking

The following list groups U.S. universities by the level of publicly visible RISC-V integration: confirmed coursework/lab use, active teaching materials or project-based use, and research-active institutions with plausible curriculum integration.
